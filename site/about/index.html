About

When’s the fight?

FightWhen answers that in one place: every big boxing, UFC, MMA, kickboxing and bare knuckle fight, in your own time zone, with where to watch it, the full card, the tale of the tape and the news around it.

How it stays up to date

  • Schedules come from promoters’ own sites and public event pages. A date or time goes live only when two sources agree; anything less is checked by a person first.
  • Breaking changes like withdrawals and replacements are picked up from news headlines within the hour and checked before they go live.
  • Fighter stats and records come from public sources, and each number must match the source’s own words. If sources disagree, we leave it blank rather than guess.
  • News is written from fighters’ and promoters’ own interviews and videos, and every story credits and links its source.
